Vezgo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Vezgo ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Vezgo mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our app:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Vehicle information
  • Pickup address
  • Photos taken by drivers (vehicle condition photos)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide vehicle inspection booking services
  • Assign drivers to pick up and return your vehicle
  • Send notifications about your booking status
  • Process payments for our services
  • Improve our app and services

Payment Information

Payments are processed through Grow by Meshulam. We do not store your payment card information. All payment data is handled securely by our payment provider.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information.

Photos

Vehicle photos taken by our drivers are stored securely and used only to document the condition of your vehicle before and after the inspection service. Photos are automatically deleted after 1 month.

Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications to update you on your booking status. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase - Database and authentication
  • Firebase - Push notifications
  • Grow by Meshulam - Payment processing

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: vezgov@gmail.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

This privacy policy was created for Vezgo vehicle inspection app, operating in Israel.
