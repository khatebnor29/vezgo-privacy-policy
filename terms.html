The service

Vezgo lets vehicle owners in Israel book the annual vehicle test (טסט), mechanic work, car wash, wheel services and similar services, including collection of the vehicle from an address you choose and its return. Vezgo arranges and coordinates these services; the test itself is carried out by a licensed testing centre and repairs by the garage performing the work.

  • For mechanic work, the garage inspects the vehicle and sends a final price. No repair is carried out before you approve that price in the app.

  • Someone must be present to hand over the vehicle and its keys at the agreed time and address, and to receive it on return.
